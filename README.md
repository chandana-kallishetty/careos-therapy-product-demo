\# CareOS



\### A human-centered therapy matching and care experience prototype



CareOS is an independent product-engineering prototype exploring how a therapy platform could make the journey from \*\*“I think I need support”\*\* to \*\*“I found someone I feel comfortable talking to”\*\* simpler, warmer, and more transparent.



This project was built as an independent prototype while exploring the Engineering Intern opportunity at Mindbun.



\## Why I built it



Therapy platforms have an interesting product problem: finding a therapist is not only a search problem.



A user may need help with:



\- understanding what kind of support they are looking for

\- finding someone who fits their preferences

\- scheduling an initial conversation

\- deciding whether the first therapist feels right

\- changing direction when the fit isn't right

\- getting help with the operational details along the way



CareOS explores that complete journey rather than treating therapist discovery as a simple directory.
