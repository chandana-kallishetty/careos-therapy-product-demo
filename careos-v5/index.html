Simple by design

Start slowly. Decide when you're ready.

The product keeps the first steps low-pressure. You don't need to know exactly what kind of therapy you need before you begin.

01

Tell us what matters

A short, friendly intake captures your concerns, preferences, language and availability.

02

Meet your match

A matching psychologist helps connect you with a therapist who may suit your needs.

03

Have a free intro

Ask questions, understand their approach and notice how the conversation feels.

04

Start when ready

Book a session only when you're comfortable continuing with the therapist.

05

Change direction

If it doesn't feel right, request another match. The journey doesn't end there.

What makes the experience calmer

Less searching. More support.

These are product principles for the prototype, informed by publicly visible patterns across therapy platforms.

♡

Human-led, not algorithm-led

AI can help structure information and surface operational signals. It should not diagnose someone or autonomously decide their treatment.

↺

Re-matching is normal

Not every good therapist is the right therapist for every person. The interface makes changing direction feel like a normal part of the journey.

◷

Scheduling without friction

Show only useful available times, keep reminders gentle, and connect booking with the rest of the care journey.

☏

A real person behind the process

A care team can see operational issues such as rescheduling, follow-ups and re-match requests without exposing unnecessary clinical detail.
